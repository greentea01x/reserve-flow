## What is good

- The two exclusion constraints are a strong, appropriately database-native design. They correctly allow pending-vs-pending while preventing live double booking and pending-vs-live overlap.
- Half-open ranges, status-guarded updates, optimistic versions, audit/outbox atomicity, idempotent sweep statements, and no-delete booking history are good foundations.
- The spec has unusually good traceability, worked API examples, test gates, Thai-first copy guidance, privacy projections, and operational runbooks.
- A single Hono service, PostgreSQL, static frontend, Caddy, and one production VM are proportionate for this organization.
- The route → service → repository convention and the separation of shared contracts from API-only infrastructure are sensible.
